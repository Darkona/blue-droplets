# Droplets of Thirst

**Droplets of Thirst** adds thirst to Minecraft. A thirst bar sits next to the hunger bar. Activity and hot places make you thirsty, and you drink water to keep going.

Water from a swamp or a puddle can make you sick. Clean it first, and then it is safe to drink. If you only want thirst, you can turn water purity off.

It is made for modpacks. It continues **[Thirst Was Taken](https://github.com/ghen-git/Thirst-Mod)** by **ghen**.

This is the **Minecraft 26.2** version, for **NeoForge 26.2.0.88 or newer**. The [wiki](https://github.com/Darkona/droplets-of-thirst/wiki) describes the newest version of the mod. Thirst, water purity, effects, config files, commands and the API are the same in this version. The wiki page [Minecraft 26.2](https://github.com/Darkona/droplets-of-thirst/wiki/Minecraft-26.2) lists what is different here. The [changelog](CHANGELOG.md) lists the changes.

## Mod compatibility

All of these mods are optional.

- **Reliquary** 2.0 or newer: the Emperor's Chalice hydrates like a drink of pure water.
- **Serene Seasons** 26.1.2.0.4 or newer: the season changes the climate of thirst loss. Serene Seasons uses these numbers for its Minecraft 26.2 versions.
- **Jade**: shows the purity of water in cauldrons and tanks.
- **JEI** 30 or newer: pages that show what each item hydrates and how to purify water.
- **AppleSkin**: its food visuals and the thirst visuals turn on and off together.
- **Traveler's Backpack** 11.3.0 or newer: the hose drinks water with the purity of the tank.

Create, Farmer's Delight, Cold Sweat, Vampirism, Supernatural and KubeJS have no version for Minecraft 26.2, so this version has no integration with them. Without Create, cooking purifies water all the way to pure.

## Other thirst mods

Tough As Nails, Legendary Survival Overhaul, Homeostatic, Survive, Thirst Was Taken, Thirst Was Reclaimed and Thirst Was Taken 2 have their own thirst. With one of them installed, the game shows a warning, and both thirst systems run. Thirst Was Taken has no version for Minecraft 26.2, so this version does not load its worlds or config files.
