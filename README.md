# Droplets of Thirst

**Droplets of Thirst** adds thirst to Minecraft. A thirst bar sits next to the hunger bar. Activity and hot places make you thirsty, and you drink water to keep going.

Water from a swamp or a puddle can make you sick. Clean it first, and then it is safe to drink. If you only want thirst, you can turn water purity off.

It is made for modpacks and works with many popular mods. It continues **[Thirst Was Taken](https://github.com/ghen-git/Thirst-Mod)** by **ghen**.

This is the **Minecraft 1.21.1** version, for **NeoForge 21.1.219 or newer**. This version works with more mods than any other version. The [wiki](https://github.com/Darkona/droplets-of-thirst/wiki) describes the newest version of the mod. Thirst, water purity, effects, config files and commands are the same in this version. The wiki page [Minecraft 1.21.1](https://github.com/Darkona/droplets-of-thirst/wiki/Minecraft-1.21.1) describes the extra integrations and their config. The [changelog](CHANGELOG.md) lists the changes.

## Mod compatibility

All of these mods are optional.

- **Create** 6.0.6 or newer: the Sand Filter purifies water that you pump through it. Fans, heated basins, spouts, drains, pipes and goggles keep or raise the purity of water.
- **Farmer's Delight** and its addons, among them Extra Delight, Farm & Charm, HerbalBrews and Brewery: drink values for their items, a minimum water purity for their recipes that use water, and water purification in the cooking pot. Kettles refuse dirty water, and taps, sinks and wells give water with the purity of the world.
- **Cold Sweat** 2.4 or newer: the body temperature controls thirst loss, and the waterskin holds water with a purity.
- **Vampirism** and **Supernatural**: vampires get thirsty, and only blood quenches their thirst.
- **Serene Seasons** 10.1 or newer: the season changes the climate of thirst loss.
- **Reliquary**: the Emperor's Chalice hydrates like a drink of pure water.
- **Jade**: shows the purity of water in cauldrons and tanks.
- **JEI** 19.21 or newer: pages that show what each item hydrates and how to purify water.
- **AppleSkin**: its food visuals and the thirst visuals turn on and off together.
- **Traveler's Backpack** 10.1 or newer: the hose drinks water with the purity of the tank.
- **KubeJS** 7: scripts can use the Droplets of Thirst API. The examples are in [examples/kubejs](examples/kubejs).

## Thirst Was Taken

Worlds and config files from Thirst Was Taken load with the thirst of each player and the purity of their water. Do not install Thirst Was Taken or Thirst Was Reclaimed together with Droplets of Thirst: the game does not start.

Tough As Nails, Legendary Survival Overhaul, Homeostatic, Survive and Thirst Was Taken 2 have their own thirst. With one of them installed, the game shows a warning, and both thirst systems run.
