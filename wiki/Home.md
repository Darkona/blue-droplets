# Blue Droplets

**Blue Droplets** adds a thirst bar and water purity to Minecraft, built to fit modpacks. It is the continuation of [Thirst Was Taken](https://github.com/ghen-git/Thirst-Mod) by ghen, for NeoForge 1.21.1 and for Minecraft 1.20.1 (one jar for Forge and NeoForge).

- [Configuration](Configuration): every config file and option, and how to play with thirst only (no water purity).
- [Modpack makers](Modpack-Makers): drink values, water purity in the world, tags, purification recipes and datapacks.
- [Mod developers](Mod-Developers): the public API and events.
- [Minecraft 1.20.1](Minecraft-1.20.1): what is different in the 1.20.1 version.

Downloads, the changelog and the source code are on the [GitHub repository](https://github.com/Darkona/blue-droplets).
