# Changelog

Changes by feature, newest version first. Upstream issues are cited as `thirst#NN` (ghen-git/Thirst-Mod).

## 26.3-1.0.0 (Minecraft 26.3, NeoForge), unreleased

The 26.3 port has every feature of the 26.2 port (next section) except the Reliquary integration, with the differences below. The jar is `droplets-of-thirst-26.3-1.0.0.jar`, for Minecraft 26.3 and NeoForge 26.3.0.36-beta only.

### Minecraft 26.3

- Thirst, quenched, dehydration, hand drinking, water purity, poured water, cauldrons, cooking purification, effects, config, data maps, commands and the API behave as on 26.2, with the same config keys (except Reliquary's, below). `[26.3]`
- Potion of Quenchness: Minecraft 26.3 brews potions from data recipes, so the three mixes (awkward potion and prismarine crystals, then redstone or glowstone dust) are now 15 `minecraft:brewing` recipes in `data/droplets_of_thirst/recipe/brewing`, one per mix and container plus the gunpowder and dragon's breath container changes. A data pack can change or remove them. `[26.3]`
- `gameplay.toml` `effects.quenchnessPotion` acts through the recipes' load condition `droplets_of_thirst:quenchness_potion`: a change applies when data packs load (a restart or `/reload`), and only the server's value counts. `[26.3]`
- Chest loot: the loot tables and global loot modifiers use Minecraft 26.3's loot format (`modifier` in place of `functions`, a single `condition` in place of `conditions`, `type` as the function and condition key). Data packs that add Droplets of Thirst loot in the old format lose their functions and conditions without an error; a global loot modifier in the old format loses its `loot_table_id` condition and adds its table to every loot table. `scripts/loot/convert.py` converts such files. `[26.3]`
- The built-in data packs (presets and purification packs) use data pack format 121. `[26.3]`

### Mod compatibility (26.3)

- Built and tested with Jade 26.3.1, JEI 31.8.0.48, AppleSkin 3.0.10, Serene Seasons 26.1.2.0.7 (its Minecraft 26.3 build), GlitchCore 26.3.0.0.3 and Traveler's Backpack 11.4.1 for Minecraft 26.3. The integrations work as on 26.2. `[26.3]`
- The biome climate of thirst loss uses vanilla's height-adjusted biome temperature, with and without Serene Seasons, so thirst loss falls with height the same either way; before, only Serene Seasons cooled it with height and the formula otherwise read the flat base temperature. `[26.3]`
- Known upstream incompatibility: NeoForge 26.3.0.37-beta renamed the config types (`ModConfig.Type.COMMON`/`SERVER` became `LOCAL`/`SYNCED`), and JEI 31.8.0.48 and Traveler's Backpack 11.4.1 crash on it (`NoSuchFieldError`). This build targets NeoForge 26.3.0.36-beta, the last build before the rename, and refuses 26.3.0.37-beta and newer. Moving to the new config types waits for JEI and Traveler's Backpack builds that load there. `[26.3]`

### Not in this version (26.3)

- Reliquary has no build for Minecraft 26.3: its integration (Emperor's Chalice drink values and the cooldown) moves to `src/disabled` and the `compat.toml` section `reliquary` (`emperorChaliceCooldown`) is gone. It comes back with its own port when Reliquary publishes a 26.3 build. `[26.3]`
- KubeJS, Create, Farmer's Delight and its addons, Cold Sweat, Supernatural and Vampirism still have no Minecraft 26.3 build; their integrations stay in `src/disabled`. `[26.3]`
- Nothing from Thirst Was Taken is migrated: it has no build for Minecraft 26.3, so the `thirst:` id aliases, the `thirst:purity` conversion (with its two runtime mixins into `ItemStack` and `FluidStack`) and the copy of the old `config/thirst/` and single-file configs are gone. Thirst Was Taken and Thirst Was Reclaimed (mod id `thirst`) are no longer marked incompatible but discouraged, like the other thirst mods, and Thirst Was Taken 2 (mod id `thirstwastaken2`) is discouraged too: the game starts with a warning and both thirst systems run side by side. `[26.3]`

### For developers (26.3)

- Built with ModDevGradle 2.0.148 on Gradle 9.7.1 and Java 25, against NeoForge 26.3.0.36-beta (FML 12). `scripts/brewing/generate.py` writes the brewing recipes; `scripts/loot/convert.py` converts loot tables and global loot modifiers to the 26.3 format. `[26.3]`
- Game tests (`./gradlew runGameTestServer`): 57 without optional mods and 63 with `-PwithCompat`. New `data_tests`: a Potion of Quenchness brewed in a brewing stand, the recipes of every container, the config switch on the recipes' load condition, and the chest loot (functions applied, modifiers only on their own chest). Minecraft 26.3 runs game tests at y=4 instead of y=-59, above the test world's sea level. `[26.3]`

## 26.1-1.0.0 (Minecraft 26.1, NeoForge), unreleased

The 26.1 port has every feature of 1.21.1-1.0.0 (next section) that does not depend on a mod without a 26.1 build, with the differences below. The jar is `droplets-of-thirst-26.1-1.0.0.jar`, for Minecraft 26.1.2 and NeoForge 26.1.2.109 or newer.

### Minecraft 26.1

- Thirst, quenched, dehydration, hand drinking, water purity, poured water, cauldrons, cooking purification, effects, config, data maps and commands behave as on 1.21.1, with the same config keys (except those of dropped integrations, below) and the same data formats for drinks, biomes and dimensions. `[26.1]`
- Food hydrates where the food component feeds the player (Minecraft 26.1 eats every food item through its food component; `Player#eat` no longer exists). Water containers and drinks without food values hydrate when they are finished, as before. `[26.1]`
- The terracotta water bowl is drunk like a water bottle and hands the empty bowl back into the inventory, or drops it when the inventory is full. Water bottles do the same now in vanilla, so Droplets of Thirst no longer patches the potion item for it. `[26.1]`
- Being too thirsty to sprint also stops a sprint in progress: Minecraft 26.1 checks it every tick, as it does for hunger. Flying and riding still allow sprinting. `[26.1]`
- The Nether multiplier and the hot climate of `hotDirtyWater` follow the environment attribute `minecraft:gameplay/water_evaporates` (the 26.1 form of the old `ultrawarm` dimension flag), so a biome or dimension that sets it counts as the Nether. `[26.1]`
- Water in a cauldron seen through NeoForge's fluid capability, and the water of a bucket, carry their purity through NeoForge's new transfer API; pumps and pipes that take the reported water from a cauldron take it with the cauldron purity. `[26.1]`
- Tints: water bottles are tinted by purity through vanilla's potion tint; the terracotta water bowl through its own item model tint `droplets_of_thirst:water_purity`, which now colours its liquid layer (on 1.21.1 the tint fell on the bowl layer). `[26.1]`
- Data: recipes, packs and item models use the 26.1 formats (custom ingredients with `neoforge:ingredient_type`, `min_format`/`max_format` in `pack.mcmeta`, item model definitions in `assets/droplets_of_thirst/items/`). Each chest loot modifier carries the `droplets_of_thirst:loot_config` condition; NeoForge 26.1 loads every file under `loot_modifiers` without a list. Cooking water reaches pure (5) with no cap of 4, since there is no Create. `[26.1]`
- The drink values, item tags and effect tags of mods without a 26.1 build are not in the jar (see below); the drinks data map keeps vanilla, Droplets of Thirst and Reliquary. `[26.1]`
- The biome climate of thirst loss uses vanilla's height-adjusted biome temperature, with and without Serene Seasons, so thirst loss falls with height the same either way; before, only Serene Seasons cooled it with height and the formula otherwise read the flat base temperature. `[26.1]`
- OWN depletion mode: a spear's stab costs thirst like an attack, where vanilla charges it hunger; stabs post no attack event, so before they cost nothing. `[26.1]`

- The mod is now **Droplets of Thirst** (mod id `droplets_of_thirst`, package `com.darkona.dropletsofthirst`), a continuation of ghen's Thirst Was Taken. The jar is `droplets-of-thirst-1.21.1-1.0.0.jar` (`modid-<minecraft>-<version>`, with a hyphen: mod ids cannot have one). `[1.21.1]`
- All ids moved from `thirst:` to `droplets_of_thirst:` (items, blocks, effects, recipes, loot tables, loot modifiers, damage type, the `loot_config` condition, translation keys). Datapacks and resource packs that target `thirst:` ids need updating. `[1.21.1]`
- The command is now `/droplets_of_thirst`; `/thirst` still works as an alias. `[1.21.1]`
- Thirst Was Taken and Thirst Was Reclaimed (mod id `thirst`) are marked incompatible: the game refuses to start with both installed and explains why. `[1.21.1]`
- Credits now name ghen's Thirst Was Taken as the original mod. `[1.21.1]`
- Worlds from Thirst Was Taken load without losing anything: old `thirst:` items, blocks, block entities (Sand Filter), effects, the purity component and the player thirst data are mapped to the new ids and saved under them from then on. `[1.21.1]`
- Player thirst, quenched and the per-player "thirst enabled" flag and water purity on items and fluids all carry over from Thirst Was Taken saves. Cauldron purity does not: cauldrons no longer store one (see Bug fixes). `[1.21.1]`
- Config files moved to `config/droplets_of_thirst/`. On first start, if that folder does not exist and `config/thirst/` does, the old files are copied over and a warning is logged. Modpacks that ship `defaultconfigs/thirst/` must rename it to `defaultconfigs/droplets_of_thirst/`. `[1.21.1]`
- Added an MIT `LICENSE` that keeps the original Thirst Was Taken copyright notice; it is also shipped inside the jar. `[1.21.1]`
- New English README: credits, migration notes, compatibility and version roadmap. `[1.21.1]`
- Temporary placeholder logo (a blue droplet) replaces the Thirst Was Taken logo until the final artwork is ready. `[1.21.1]`

### Water purity

- Water purity has six levels: contaminated (0), dirty (1), murky (2), acceptable (3), clean (4) and pure (5). They are named in the public API by `PurityLevel` (`DropletsAPI.MIN_PURITY` and `MAX_PURITY` are 0 and 5), in tooltips, Jade, Create goggles and the Sand Filter tooltip, each with its own colour. Water with no purity stored reads as acceptable (`defaultPurity` 3). `[1.21.1]`
- Drinking effects by default: contaminated gives Nausea and Hunger and 40% Poison, which blocks hydration; dirty 60% Nausea and Hunger and 15% Poison; murky 25% Nausea and Hunger; acceptable 5%; clean and pure nothing. Pure water also hydrates more (`[pureWater]`, see Config). `[1.21.1]`
- World water starts dirty (`worldWaterBasePurity` 1), running water is one level better, and height adds up to three levels (`altitudeBands`), both above sea level (mountains) and below it (caves). Ocean water is always contaminated. Biomes change the base and the cap: see Datapacks. `[1.21.1]`
- A water cauldron gives murky water (2), or clean water (4) over a heat source. Cooking adds levels: a campfire one per cooking, a furnace or a smoker two, up to pure without Create and up to clean with it. With Create, pure water only comes from the Sand Filter (`sandFilterMaxPurity` 5); fans and heated basins add one level up to clean, and compacting a cactus gives acceptable water. `[1.21.1]`
- **Poured water keeps its purity.** Pouring sea water into a meadow and filling a bucket there no longer gives the meadow's water back. A water source poured with a bucket (by a player or a dispenser), placed through NeoForge's `FluidUtil.tryPlaceFluid` (used by many mods' containers) or by a Create open pipe end is remembered with the purity of the water poured (or `defaultPurity` when it had none). Filling a bottle, a bowl or a bucket there, drinking by hand, Create pipes and hose pulleys read that purity. The infinite source that forms between two poured sources takes the worst purity beside it, and picking up a poured source hands its purity to the sources beside it, so the one that refills its place is not clean either. A remembered position that no longer holds a water source is ignored and forgotten when read. It is one small map per chunk, saved with the chunk and never sent to clients; nothing runs per tick. `[1.21.1]`
- Thirst Was Taken had four levels. Its saves and config files move to the new ones: dirty to contaminated (0), slightly dirty to dirty (1), acceptable to acceptable (3), purified to pure (5). This covers the purity of items and fluids (`thirst:purity`, converted as the item or fluid loads), `defaultPurity`, `worldWaterBasePurity`, `saltWaterPurity` and the old effect percentages. `[1.21.1]`

### Mod compatibility

- API: checked with KubeJS 7 (1.21.1). Example scripts in `examples/kubejs` (thirst, purity, events, a registered drink) run in the game tests with `-PwithKubeJS`. New unambiguous methods `DropletsAPI.getItemPurity`, `getFluidPurity`, `withItemPurity` and `withFluidPurity`, because scripts cannot choose between the `ItemStack` and `FluidStack` overloads. See the KubeJS section of Mod developers. `[1.21.1]`
- Farmer's Delight addons and Let's Do mods (optional; tested with Extra Delight 2.6.6, Farm & Charm 1.1.26, HerbalBrews 1.1.4, Brewery 2.1.11, Fruits Delight 1.2.14, Cultural Delights 0.18.1, Expanded Delight 0.1.4, Miner's Delight 1.4.5, Corn Delight 1.2.10, Rustic Delight 1.7.1, Crabber's Delight 1.3.1, End's Delight 2.6.1 and My Nether's Delight 1.10.4.1): kettles boil the water poured in, so they refuse dirty water. The HerbalBrews tea kettle leaves water below `compat.toml` `delight.kettleMinPurity` (default 2: murky or better) in its water slot, and the Brewery brewing stations refuse it with a message. The stations are in the new block tag `droplets_of_thirst:rejects_dirty_water`; datapacks can add other blocks that are filled by clicking with water. `[1.21.1]`
- Taps and sinks of Extra Delight, the sink of the Let's Do mods (Farm & Charm's sink block) and the Farm & Charm timber well give water with the purity of the world's water where they stand, as if it came from a source block there, instead of water without a purity (read as acceptable). Covers buckets, bottles and pipes that drain a tap or a sink. New `compat.toml` `delight.worldPurityWaterSources` (default `true`). `[1.21.1]`
- Expanded Delight 0.1.4 disables Farmer's Delight's `melon_juice` and Farmer's Respite's `melon_juice_from_water` with a `neoforge:never` condition that NeoForge 21.1 does not know, which logged two recipe parsing errors. The `clean_water_cooking` datapack now overrides both with the same recipe under a `not mod_loaded expandeddelight` condition: with Expanded Delight the recipes stay off (the Juicer makes the juice) and the log is clean, without it they work as before. `[1.21.1]`
- Extra Delight 2.6.6 disables a `minecraft:dough` recipe with the same `neoforge:never` condition, which NeoForge 21.1 does not know, so it logged a parsing error on every load. The same built-in pack overrides that id with `neoforge:false`: nothing changes in play and the log stays clean. `[1.21.1]`
- Reliquary (optional, `[2.0,)`; tested with 2.0.80): drinking from the Emperor's Chalice hydrates like a drink of pure water (thirst 4, quenched 5, purity 5, no extra pure water bonus), through the `droplets_of_thirst:drinks` data map; its own food and damage stay as Reliquary has them. The Infernal Chalice does not hydrate. New `compat.toml` option `reliquary.emperorChaliceCooldown` (default `0`, off): ticks before the chalice can be used again after a drink. `[1.21.1]`
- Extra Delight: water bottles keep their purity when they go into its tanks and machines as 250 mB of water, and bottles filled from its tanks take the water's purity. Before, a dirty bottle entered a tank as water without a purity. `[1.21.1]`
- Miner's Delight: the water cup (`minersdelight:water_cup`) carries a purity like a bottle. It is in `droplets_of_thirst:purity_containers`, so it shows the purity in its tooltip and goes through the cauldron and Create like a bottle: filled from a water source (the copper cup picks up water on its own, so a small mixin gives it what buckets get), it takes the purity of that water, including poured water; filled at a cauldron, the cauldron's purity; emptied into the world, the source it leaves keeps the purity. Miner's Delight's own Create recipes for the cup sit in a `recipes` folder with `forge:` conditions, which 1.21 does not read, so Create never filled or emptied a cup; the new recipes `compat/create/water_cup` and `compat/create/copper_cup_from_emptying` (1000 mB) do, and the spout and the item drain hand the purity on. The cup is not a drink (it has no drinking action), so it has no drink values. `[1.21.1]`
- Cultural Delights: the vat holds no fluid and gives no water back; water only goes in as a bucket (`c:buckets/water`) in its aging recipes, and the bucket it returns is empty. So there is no purity to store or pass on, and the rule is in the recipes: with `clean_water_cooking` the cold aging recipes (beer, wine, whiskey and the rest) take only water of purity 3 or better, and a game test checks it against the vat's own recipe matching. `[1.21.1]`
- Cold Sweat was never detected on 1.21.1: its mod id is `cold_sweat`, and Droplets of Thirst looked for `coldsweat`, so body temperature never drove thirst and never counted for hot dirty water. Both work now. Cold Sweat must be 2.4 or newer when installed; built against 2.4.3.1. `[1.21.1]`
- Cold Sweat: with `compat.toml` `coldsweat.useBodyTemperature` (default `true`), the climate multiplier of thirst now comes from the new `coldsweat.bodyTemperatureCurve` (default `["-100,0.8", "0,1.0", "50,1.3", "100,2.0", "150,3.0"]`: comfortable is 1, burning is twice the thirst). Before, the body temperature divided by 100 was fed into the biome formula, which made a comfortable body temperature halve thirst. The dimension's thirst multiplier and the Nether multiplier still come first. `[1.21.1]`
- Cold Sweat: drinking water can cool the body, through Cold Sweat's own temperature API: new `compat.toml` `coldsweat.drinkCooling` (default `0.0`, off) and `coldsweat.drinkCoolingTicks` (default `0`: a one-time drop of the body temperature; more: a lower base temperature for that long, like Cold Sweat's cold foods). It counts water containers, drinking by hand and the Traveler's Backpack hose; Cold Sweat's waterskin keeps its own water temperature instead. `[1.21.1]`
- Cold Sweat waterskin: drinking from it hydrates like a water drink (thirst 6, quenched 3, in `droplets_of_thirst:drinks`), with the purity of its water and its effects (thirst#210). Filling it from a water source, a water cauldron or a tank gives it the purity of that water. The furnace, campfire and smoker purification packs purify it like a bottle (thirst#267); it comes out full. An emptied waterskin (last sip, pouring, crafting) loses its purity, so it stacks with the other empty ones again. Both are small runtime mixins into Cold Sweat (`WaterskinItem.getFilledItem` and `FilledWaterskinItem.getCraftingRemainingItem`), because its waterskin copies every component between the empty and the filled item and only handles Thirst Was Taken's purity. Not done: Cold Sweat's boiler does not purify water for Droplets of Thirst (thirst#160), because it only knows Thirst Was Taken and changing that would mean patching Cold Sweat. `[1.21.1]`
- Create fans and basins purify water, never past clean (4), so pure water (5) still only comes from the Sand Filter: washing with a fan (water in front of it) takes water bottles and terracotta water bowls up one level per pass, up to clean; water buckets are left out, because Create hands back a bucket's crafting remainder next to the result. A mixer over a heated basin (Blaze Burner) takes water up one level per mix, up to clean, 250 mB per mix. Smoking and blasting fans already used the smoker and furnace purification recipes; with the smoker pack on, a blasting fan burns water containers like any food, as Create does. The new recipes carry `droplets_of_thirst:purity_enabled` and do not load with purity off. `[1.21.1]`

- The thirst loss multiplier from climate (biome temperature and humidity, or Cold Sweat body temperature; Nether), Fire Protection and Fire Resistance is computed once per second per player (players are spread over different ticks), and right away after changing armor, gaining or losing an effect, changing dimension or respawning. Thirst and quenched drunk past full (bottles, food, hand drinking, rain, `DropletsAPI.drink`; not Quenchness) add up as overflow, which drains by `overhydration.decayPerSecond` (1.0) points per second. At `overhydration.threshold` (20, about two water bottles while full) the player gets the new harmful **Overhydrated** effect (`droplets_of_thirst:overhydrated`, 10% slower movement per level) for `durationTicks` (400), one level more per half threshold past it or when it is already active (at most III), plus 10 seconds of Nausea (`overhydration.nausea`). The thirst bar turns greyish blue while it lasts (`Bar Colors.overhydrated`). Disable it with `overhydration.enabled = false` (`gameplay.toml`). `[1.21.1]`
- Dirty water keeps giving the vanilla Nausea, Hunger and Poison from the purity lists in `purity.toml`; there is no separate water sickness effect. `[1.21.1]`

- Water rebalanced to 250 mB = 2 droplets: a water bottle gives thirst 4 and quenched 5 (was 6 and 8), so pure water gives 6 and 8. `[1.21.1]`

Out-of-range values are reset to the default with a warning. `[1.21.1]`

The documented "-32 for aquatic biomes" never existed and is gone; use biome tags or `droplets_of_thirst:biome_water` instead. `[1.21.1]`
- New `stillWaterPurificationAmount` (default 0) in `common.toml`. `[1.21.1]`
- Removed the unused `kettlePurificationLevels`, `fermentationMoldingThreshold` and `fermentationMoldingHarshness` options (sections "Purification levels" and "Fermentation levels" of `common.toml`); they never did anything. Kettle and fermentation purification will come back as datapack recipes. `[1.21.1]`
- **Config files split by concern**: `common.toml`, `item_settings.toml`, `container.toml` and `keyword.toml` are replaced by `gameplay.toml`, `purity.toml`, `items.toml` and `compat.toml` (plus the unchanged `client.toml`), with shorter key names grouped in sections. On first start the values of the old files are copied to the new ones once and the old files are renamed to `*.toml.old`, with a warning in the log. Modpacks that ship `defaultconfigs/` must use the new names; see `wiki/Configuration.md` for the full table. `[1.21.1]`
- Editing a common config file while a server runs now applies everywhere right away: the drink tables are rebuilt and sent again, and every player's thirst loss multiplier and synced rules are refreshed (before, only `/reload` resent the tables and the multiplier waited up to a second). `[1.21.1]`
- On a dedicated server, clients now use the server's `hand.enabled` (drinking by hand) and `waterBottleStackSize`; before, a client with a different local value could not drink by hand or saw water bottles stack differently from the server. `[1.21.1]`
- New `purity.enabled` (`purity.toml`, default `true`): when `false`, water has no purity at all: nothing stores a purity component, tooltips, Jade and Create goggles show none, drinking never gives purity effects, and the purification recipes are not loaded. Synced to clients. It also covers chest loot drinks (no purity), the creative tab (each water container once instead of once per purity), Create's cactus compacting (plain water), the Sand Filter (passes water through unchanged), Jade on the server side and `DrinkEvent` (reports `NO_PURITY`). `[1.21.1]`
- **Purity effects are configurable lists** (`purity.toml`, section `effects`, one list per purity, `contaminated` to `pure`: `"effect_id,durationTicks,amplifier,chancePercent[,blocksHydration]"`): any effect, duration, level and chance, replacing the eight fixed nausea/poison percentages. Defaults keep the old effects with the chances of the six levels: hunger 30 s, poison 10 s (blocks hydration) and nausea, now 10 s instead of 5 s. Vanilla only builds up the Nausea screen distortion while more than 3 seconds are left, so 5 seconds was barely a wobble; 10 seconds nearly reaches its full strength. Each drink resets it to 10 seconds, it does not add up. Old percentages are converted once, with these durations. `[1.21.1]`

New: `mode` (`MIRROR_FOOD`, the default, follows hunger exhaustion as before; `OWN` counts sprinting, swimming, jumping, attacking, mining, damage and food regeneration itself with vanilla's numbers, so mods that reset or change hunger exhaustion no longer affect thirst, thirst#271), `basalPerTick`, climate `formula` (`LEGACY` or `CURVE` with temperature and humidity curves), `legacyHarshness`, rain, thunder, day, night, sun, in-water, underwater and altitude multipliers, `ridingMultiplier` (0 = as before), `sleepingMultiplier`, and the Fire Protection amount and cap. `[1.21.1]`
- Riding or having Nourishment (and similar effects) no longer stores up thirst loss that is then applied all at once when it ends, and the Hunger effect no longer lowers thirst exhaustion while riding. Nausea thirst loss is no longer mistaken for health regeneration by `regeneration.depletesThirst`/`climateDependent`. `[1.21.1]`
- New `drinking.canFillFromFlowingWater` (`gameplay.toml`, default `true`, sent to clients): when `false`, glass bottles and terracotta bowls can only be filled from water source blocks, as in vanilla; buckets are unaffected (closes thirst#238). `[1.21.1]`
- New `hand.effects` (`gameplay.toml`, default `true`): drinking water by hand swings the arm and splashes water particles where you drink, visible to nearby players; `false` keeps the sound only (closes thirst#256). `[1.21.1]`
- New `compat.toml` `coldsweat.useBodyTemperature` (default `true`, as before). `[1.21.1]`
- More gameplay numbers are configurable in `gameplay.toml` (defaults = current behaviour): `exhaustionPerPoint`, `nauseaPerTick`; dehydration damage `amount`, `intervalTicks`, minimum health per difficulty and `canKill`; regeneration thresholds (`fullRegenMinThirst`, `slowRegenMinThirst`, `slowRegenIntervalTicks`, `hungerRegenMinThirst`) and Peaceful regeneration; `sprint.minThirst` (sent to clients); rain drinking pitch, interval and amounts; `death.respawnThirst`/`respawnQuenched` (-1 keeps the values the player died with). `[1.21.1]`
- Config problems that NeoForge cannot see per key (unknown effect ids, unknown item or tag ids in `items.toml`, overlapping altitude bands, curve points out of order, invalid keyword patterns) are logged as a single warning on each world load, `/reload` or config change (the separate "Skipped N config entries" and "Ignoring invalid keyword pattern" warnings are gone). New commands for operators: `/droplets_of_thirst config check`, `/droplets_of_thirst debug exhaustion [player]` (every factor of thirst loss) and `/droplets_of_thirst debug purity` (how the purity of the water you look at is made). `[1.21.1]`

- **Recipe inference** (`items.toml`, section `inference`, **off by default**): estimates thirst and quenched of items with no values from any other source from the ingredients of the recipes that make them (any recipe type with item ingredients, averaged per ingredient, per-category multipliers, caps, maximum depth, loops cut, blacklist of items, tags, namespaces and recipe types). Explicit values always win. Estimated items show "(est.)" in the tooltip; `/droplets_of_thirst infer <item>` explains an estimate. Computed on the server at world load, `/reload` and config change and sent to clients. `[1.21.1]`

### API

- Negative (salty) values: `ThirstValues.saltiness()` and `DrinkEvent#getSaltiness()`/`isSalty()` give the thirst points an item removes (2 points = 1 droplet on the HUD). The `drinks` data map codec now allows thirst -20..20 and quenched ≥ -20. Network protocol version `0.1.11` (a stack-dependent entry in `droplets_of_thirst:thirst_values` is now marked with thirst `Integer.MIN_VALUE` instead of -1). `[1.21.1]`
- Eating and drinking are now separate hooks. Food (items without the drink animation that are not water containers, also when another mod calls `Player#eat`, block foods and the new `DropletsAPI.eat(player, thirst, quenched)`) posts the new `EatEvent.Pre` (cancel, or change thirst and quenched) and `EatEvent.Post`, and changes thirst with the new `ThirstChangeEvent.Cause.EAT`; drinks (drink animation: potions, milk, honey bottles, most modded drinks; water containers; hand drinking; `DropletsAPI.drink`) keep `DrinkEvent` and `Cause.DRINK`. Both events have `getSaltiness()`/`isSalty()`. Food no longer rolls purity effects: a `purity` in `droplets_of_thirst:drinks` only applies to drinks. `[1.21.1]`
- New cancelable `OverhydrationEvent`: posted before Overhydrated is applied, with the overflow; change the duration or the amplifier, or cancel. `[1.21.1]`
- `DropletsAPI.registerWaveEffect(effect)` makes the thirst bar wave while the local player has that effect (client side), for positive thirst effects. `[1.21.1]`
- `DropletsAPI.registerBarStyle(id, predicate, rgb, priority)` tints the thirst bar while the predicate holds for the local player (client side); see the wiki page Mod Developers. Removed `ThirstBarRenderer.cancelRender` and the copied GlitchCore `RenderGuiEvent`; the bar is the GUI layer `droplets_of_thirst:thirst_level`, which other mods can move or cancel with NeoForge's `RenderGuiLayerEvent`. `[1.21.1]`
- New wiki page Mod Developers: dependency setup, soft-dependency guard, reading, changing, registering and events, with examples. `[1.21.1]`
- Events on the NeoForge bus (`com.darkona.dropletsofthirst.api.event`), server side and only when something happens, never every tick: `ThirstChangeEvent.Pre` (cancel or change the new values) and `.Post`, with a cause (depletion, drink, rain, Peaceful, death, command, API); `DrinkEvent.Pre` (cancel or change thirst, quenched and purity) and `.Post` (with whether it hydrated), for items, drinking by hand and `DropletsAPI.drink`; `PurityEffectEvent` (cancel, or edit the rolled effects and whether the drink still hydrates); `DehydrationDamageEvent` (cancel or change the damage). Eating a drink-food no longer updates thirst on the client ahead of the server; the server's value arrives the same tick. `[1.21.1]`
- Registering from code: `DropletsAPI.registerDrink(item, thirst, quenched[, purity])`, `registerDrinkProvider(item, provider)` for values that depend on the stack's components, `registerContainer([empty,] filled)` for water containers with purity, and `registerExhaustionModifier(id, modifier)` for thirst loss rules the `droplets_of_thirst:thirst_drain` attribute cannot express (evaluated with the cached multiplier about once a second, in id order; `refreshExhaustionModifier(player)` forces it). Registered values are applied every time the tables are built; `items.toml`, `droplets_of_thirst:no_thirst` and the `droplets_of_thirst:drinks` data map still win over them, and they win over keywords and recipe estimates. `RegisterThirstValueEvent` and `WaterPurity.addContainer` are deprecated in favour of these. `/droplets_of_thirst debug exhaustion` shows the registered modifiers as "other mods". The impl class `ExhaustionModifier` is now `ExhaustionFactors`. Network protocol version `0.1.10`. `[1.21.1]`
- `DropletsAPI.view(player)` returns a live read-only `DropletsView` (thirst, quenched, exhaustion, max thirst, enabled, last exhaustion multiplier) on both sides. Server-side changes: `setThirst`, `setQuenched`, `addThirst`, `drink(player, thirst, quenched[, purity])` (rolls purity effects like a bottle) and `addExhaustion` (multiplied like the mod's own activities); on a client they do nothing and log one warning. Values and purity: `getDrinkValues(stack)` (resolved, with an `estimated` flag; `null` when the item gives nothing), `getPurity(ItemStack|FluidStack)`, `withPurity(...)` (a copy), `getWaterPurity(level, pos)`, `isPurityEnabled()`. All thirst changes (depletion, drinking, rain, Peaceful regeneration, death, `/droplets_of_thirst set`) now go through one clamped method. `[1.21.1]`
- New public API in `com.darkona.dropletsofthirst.api`, built from its own source set: it ships inside the mod jar and alone as `com.darkona.dropletsofthirst:droplets-of-thirst-api` (with a sources jar) for other mods to compile against. Start at `DropletsAPI`; see the wiki page Mod Developers. `ThirstHelper` moved to `com.darkona.dropletsofthirst.content.thirst` and is internal. `[1.21.1]`
- All classes moved from `dev.ghen.thirst` to `com.darkona.dropletsofthirst` (main class `Thirst` is now `DropletsOfThirst`) and there is no compatibility shim: addons that call Thirst Was Taken classes directly, such as Green Feathers, need a version built for Droplets of Thirst. A stable public API is planned. `[1.21.1]`

### For developers

- `./gradlew runGameTestServer -PwithDelight` runs with Farmer's Delight 1.3.4, Brewin' and Chewin', Extra Delight, Ocean's Delight, Ender's Delight, Farm & Charm, HerbalBrews, Brewery, Fruits Delight, Cultural Delights, Expanded Delight, Miner's Delight, Corn Delight, Rustic Delight, Crabber's Delight, End's Delight and My Nether's Delight (64 GameTests); it combines with `-PwithCompat`. `[1.21.1]`
- Development command `/droplets_of_thirst dev dump_items <namespace>...` (not registered in production): writes `run/delight/items.csv` with one row per item of those namespaces (`*`: every namespace with food or drinks), with its food values and effects, whether it is drunk, its tags and the thirst values Droplets of Thirst gives it today. It feeds the data scripts. `[1.21.1]`
- Boot checks: `scripts/server-boot-check.sh` starts a dedicated server in `run/bootcheck-server` (superflat world, random port), stops it with the shutdown hook and fails on any error in the log that `scripts/boot-check-known.txt` does not list; `scripts/client-boot-check.sh` boots the client headless (Xvfb + Mesa) into a copy of a superflat world, types optional commands and takes a screenshot of the HUD. Both take `GRADLE_ARGS="-PwithCompat"` and only ever stop processes they started (tagged environment, no `pkill`). New Gradle runs `runServerBootCheck` and `runBootCheck`. `[1.21.1]`
- GameTests (`./gradlew runGameTestServer`, 52 tests; with `-PwithCompat` 79): drinking and eating, quenched and salty limits, overhydration, purity effects, command results, the player save, the purity component on disk and on the network, the drinks data map codec, the sync payloads, cauldron purity and boiling, bucket purity through the fluid capability, dispensers, filling from the world, optional mods present or absent, the effect tag that pauses thirst (with Farmer's Delight), everything with `purity.enabled=false`, the content of the recipe viewer pages, the End's cold climate, the purity condition on every purity recipe, the six purity levels (effect tables, altitude bands, Thirst Was Taken mapping), poured water (buckets, bottles, dispensers, `FluidUtil`, the infinite source between two poured ones, stale entries, the chunk save), and with Create the open pipe end (also pouring), the Sand Filter (sides, stages, maximum purity), fan washing and smoking, the heated basin, spout filling and emptying, and the creative tabs, and with Cold Sweat the waterskin (drink, purity when filled, cooking), drinking cooling and the body temperature curve, and with Serene Seasons winter, the tropical dry season, biomes without seasons and the switch, and with the Delight mods (`-PwithDelight`, 58 tests) the kettles, taps, sinks, the timber well, Extra Delight bottles, the Miner's Delight water cup (with Create too, its spout and drain recipes) and the Cultural Delights vat recipes; also the pure water bonus and the item dump. They ship in the jar under `com.darkona.dropletsofthirst.gametest` and only run when `neoforge.enabledGameTestNamespaces` includes `droplets_of_thirst`. `[1.21.1]`
- Mixins no longer replace whole methods of Minecraft, NeoForge or Create, so they combine with other mods that hook the same code: bucket purity through the NeoForge fluid capability is added to the result of `FluidBucketWrapper.getFluid` and `FluidType.getBucket(FluidStack)` instead of overwriting `getFluid` and `FluidUtil.getFilledBucket`; dispensers give water containers the purity of the water in front of them from `DefaultDispenseItemBehavior.consumeWithRemainder`, so the vanilla bucket and glass bottle dispenser behaviours are no longer replaced (other mods that register their own keep working in any load order) and containers dispensed through NeoForge's `DispenseFluidContainer` get it too. The `@Redirect`s on regeneration in `FoodData.tick`, on the empty bottle in `PotionItem.finishUsingItem` and on the food level in `LocalPlayer.hasEnoughFoodToStartSprinting` are now `@WrapOperation`/`@ModifyExpressionValue`, with the same behaviour. Filling a bucket or a glass bottle from water in the world is hooked into `BucketItem.use` and `BottleItem.use` (the filled item gets the purity) instead of cancelling every right click with those items and doing vanilla's work again, so other mods' buckets and bottles that extend them get purity too; the terracotta bowl has its own item class. Drinking a terracotta water bowl hands back the empty bowl through vanilla's `ItemUtils.createFilledResult`, like a glass bottle; in creative mode you now get an empty bowl if you have none, as with bottles. Create goggles show the purity by wrapping the one call that names the fluid in `IHaveGoggleInformation.containedFluidTooltip`, instead of overwriting the whole method; the rest of the tooltip is Create's own again. `[1.21.1]`
- Build moved from NeoGradle to ModDevGradle 2.0.141 on Gradle 9.7.1, built against NeoForge 21.1.252; the minimum NeoForge version is now 21.1.219. `[1.21.1]`
