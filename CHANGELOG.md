# Changelog

## 1.18.2-1.0.0 (Minecraft 1.18.2, Forge 40), 2026-10-01

The 1.18.2 port has every feature of the 1.19.2 port (next section) and so of 1.21.1-1.0.0, with the differences below. The jar, `droplets-of-thirst-1.18.2-1.0.0.jar`, runs on Forge 40.3.12 or newer for Minecraft 1.18.2.

### Minecraft 1.18.2

- Everything the 1.19.2 section says about storage, data, dehydration damage, the creative tab and the optional datapacks also holds here. The release jar starts and stops cleanly on a dedicated Forge 1.18.2-40.3.12 server, alone and with the optional mods. `[1.18.2]`
- Worlds from Thirst Was Taken 1.18.2 (1.3.x) load like those of 1.19.2 and 1.20.1: the capability, the `thirst:` ids and the `Purity` of water containers and tanks use the same formats. `[1.18.2]`
- The default `biome_water` data map uses the biome tags that Forge 40 has: deserts and badlands by `#forge:is_sandy`, swamps by `#forge:is_swamp`, snowy biomes by `#forge:is_snowy` and mountains by `#minecraft:is_mountain`, which on 1.18.2 holds Forge's peaks and slopes. Forge 40 has no `forge:is_desert` or `forge:is_mountain`. Every vanilla biome gets the same purity as on later versions; 1.18.2 has no mangrove swamp. `[1.18.2]`
- The biome climate of thirst loss uses vanilla's height-adjusted biome temperature, with and without Serene Seasons, so thirst loss falls with height the same either way; before, only Serene Seasons cooled it with height and the formula otherwise read the flat base temperature. `[1.18.2]`
- Telling a water bottle from other potions (stack size, item colour, tooltips) no longer parses the potion's name on every call: the answer for the last name read is kept. `[1.18.2]`

### Mod compatibility (1.18.2)

- Works with Create 0.5.1.i, Jade 5.3.2, JEI 10.2.1, AppleSkin 2.5.1, Cold Sweat 2.4.3, Serene Seasons 7.0.0.15, Traveler's Backpack 7.1.49, Reliquary 2.0.19 and Farmer's Delight 1.2.3 for 1.18.2, and Vampirism 1.8.8. `[1.18.2]`

- JEI: the hydration and purification pages are the same. Droplets of Thirst is built against the JEI 9.7 API, the line most 1.18.2 mods use, and runs on JEI 9.7.2 and 10.2.1. `[1.18.2]`
- Farmer's Delight addons, data only (drink values and, where a recipe uses water, the clean water rule): Brewin' and Chewin' 1.0.1, Ocean's Delight 1.0.0, Ender's Delight 1.2.1, Miner's Delight 1.1.1, Corn Delight 1.0.6, Crabber's Delight 1.1.2 and End's Delight 1.2.1. The clean water rule: Farmer's Delight's cooking pot and wheat dough, Miner's Delight's copper pot and Corn Delight's raw tortilla need acceptable water or better (3). Miner's Delight's water cup carries a purity like a bottle. `[1.18.2]`

Vampirism 1.8.8 has no blood drinking event, so a small mixin at the head of `VampirePlayer.drinkBlood` makes every drink of blood (bites, blood bottles, blood containers, blood food) restore thirst: `compat.toml` `vampirism.thirstPerBlood` (1.0) and `vampirism.quenchedPerBlood` (1.0, times the blood's saturation); altars and commands, which fill the blood bar with the maximum amount, do not count. Items a datapack adds to the new item tag `droplets_of_thirst:blood` (empty here) hydrate vampires with their `drinks.json` values. With `MIRROR_FOOD`, hunger exhaustion that another mod empties between ticks now still counts. `[1.18.2]`

### For developers (1.18.2)

The thirst bar is the overlay "Droplets of Thirst Thirst" of Forge's `OverlayRegistry`, right above `ForgeIngameGui.FOOD_LEVEL_ELEMENT`; other mods can hide it with `OverlayRegistry.enableOverlay` or cancel it in `RenderGameOverlayEvent.PreLayer`. `[1.18.2]`

### Water purity

- Water purity has six levels: contaminated (0), dirty (1), murky (2), acceptable (3), clean (4) and pure (5). They are named in the public API by `PurityLevel` (`DropletsAPI.MIN_PURITY` and `MAX_PURITY` are 0 and 5), in tooltips, Jade, Create goggles and the Sand Filter tooltip, each with its own colour. Water with no purity stored reads as acceptable (`defaultPurity` 3). `[1.21.1]`
- Drinking effects by default: contaminated gives Nausea and Hunger and 40% Poison, which blocks hydration; dirty 60% Nausea and Hunger and 15% Poison; murky 25% Nausea and Hunger; acceptable 5%; clean and pure nothing. Pure water also hydrates more (`[pureWater]`, see Config). `[1.21.1]`
- World water starts dirty (`worldWaterBasePurity` 1), running water is one level better, and height adds up to three levels (`altitudeBands`), both above sea level (mountains) and below it (caves). Ocean water is always contaminated. Biomes change the base and the cap: see Datapacks. `[1.21.1]`
- A water cauldron gives murky water (2), or clean water (4) over a heat source. Cooking adds levels: a campfire one per cooking, a furnace or a smoker two, up to pure without Create and up to clean with it. With Create, pure water only comes from the Sand Filter (`sandFilterMaxPurity` 5); fans and heated basins add one level up to clean, and compacting a cactus gives acceptable water. `[1.21.1]`
- **Poured water keeps its purity.** Pouring sea water into a meadow and filling a bucket there no longer gives the meadow's water back. A water source poured with a bucket (by a player or a dispenser), placed through NeoForge's `FluidUtil.tryPlaceFluid` (used by many mods' containers) or by a Create open pipe end is remembered with the purity of the water poured (or `defaultPurity` when it had none). Filling a bottle, a bowl or a bucket there, drinking by hand, Create pipes and hose pulleys read that purity. The infinite source that forms between two poured sources takes the worst purity beside it, and picking up a poured source hands its purity to the sources beside it, so the one that refills its place is not clean either. A remembered position that no longer holds a water source is ignored and forgotten when read. It is one small map per chunk, saved with the chunk and never sent to clients; nothing runs per tick. `[1.21.1]`
- Thirst Was Taken had four levels. Its saves and config files move to the new ones: dirty to contaminated (0), slightly dirty to dirty (1), acceptable to acceptable (3), purified to pure (5). This covers the purity of items and fluids (`thirst:purity`, converted as the item or fluid loads), `defaultPurity`, `worldWaterBasePurity`, `saltWaterPurity` and the old effect percentages. `[1.21.1]`

New unambiguous methods `DropletsAPI.getItemPurity`, `getFluidPurity`, `withItemPurity` and `withFluidPurity`, because scripts cannot choose between the `ItemStack` and `FluidStack` overloads. See the KubeJS section of Mod developers. `[1.21.1]`
- Farmer's Delight addons and Let's Do mods (optional; works with Extra Delight 2.6.6, Farm & Charm 1.1.26, HerbalBrews 1.1.4, Brewery 2.1.11, Fruits Delight 1.2.14, Cultural Delights 0.18.1, Expanded Delight 0.1.4, Miner's Delight 1.4.5, Corn Delight 1.2.10, Rustic Delight 1.7.1, Crabber's Delight 1.3.1, End's Delight 2.6.1 and My Nether's Delight 1.10.4.1): kettles boil the water poured in, so they refuse dirty water. The HerbalBrews tea kettle leaves water below `compat.toml` `delight.kettleMinPurity` (default 2: murky or better) in its water slot, and the Brewery brewing stations refuse it with a message. The stations are in the new block tag `droplets_of_thirst:rejects_dirty_water`; datapacks can add other blocks that are filled by clicking with water. `[1.21.1]`
- Taps and sinks of Extra Delight, the sink of the Let's Do mods (Farm & Charm's sink block) and the Farm & Charm timber well give water with the purity of the world's water where they stand, as if it came from a source block there, instead of water without a purity (read as acceptable). Covers buckets, bottles and pipes that drain a tap or a sink. New `compat.toml` `delight.worldPurityWaterSources` (default `true`). `[1.21.1]`
- Expanded Delight 0.1.4 disables Farmer's Delight's `melon_juice` and Farmer's Respite's `melon_juice_from_water` with a `neoforge:never` condition that NeoForge 21.1 does not know, which logged two recipe parsing errors. The `clean_water_cooking` datapack now overrides both with the same recipe under a `not mod_loaded expandeddelight` condition: with Expanded Delight the recipes stay off (the Juicer makes the juice) and the log is clean, without it they work as before. `[1.21.1]`
- Extra Delight 2.6.6 disables a `minecraft:dough` recipe with the same `neoforge:never` condition, which NeoForge 21.1 does not know, so it logged a parsing error on every load. The same built-in pack overrides that id with `neoforge:false`: nothing changes in play and the log stays clean. `[1.21.1]`
- Reliquary (optional, `[2.0,)`; works with 2.0.80): drinking from the Emperor's Chalice hydrates like a drink of pure water (thirst 4, quenched 5, purity 5, no extra pure water bonus), through the `droplets_of_thirst:drinks` data map; its own food and damage stay as Reliquary has them. The Infernal Chalice does not hydrate. New `compat.toml` option `reliquary.emperorChaliceCooldown` (default `0`, off): ticks before the chalice can be used again after a drink. `[1.21.1]`
- Extra Delight: water bottles keep their purity when they go into its tanks and machines as 250 mB of water, and bottles filled from its tanks take the water's purity. Before, a dirty bottle entered a tank as water without a purity. `[1.21.1]`
- Miner's Delight: the water cup (`minersdelight:water_cup`) carries a purity like a bottle. It is in `droplets_of_thirst:purity_containers`, so it shows the purity in its tooltip and goes through the cauldron and Create like a bottle: filled from a water source (the copper cup picks up water on its own, so a small mixin gives it what buckets get), it takes the purity of that water, including poured water; filled at a cauldron, the cauldron's purity; emptied into the world, the source it leaves keeps the purity. Miner's Delight's own Create recipes for the cup sit in a `recipes` folder with `forge:` conditions, which 1.21 does not read, so Create never filled or emptied a cup; the new recipes `compat/create/water_cup` and `compat/create/copper_cup_from_emptying` (1000 mB) do, and the spout and the item drain hand the purity on. The cup is not a drink (it has no drinking action), so it has no drink values. `[1.21.1]`
- Cultural Delights: the vat holds no fluid and gives no water back; water only goes in as a bucket (`c:buckets/water`) in its aging recipes, and the bucket it returns is empty. So there is no purity to store or pass on, and the rule is in the recipes: with `clean_water_cooking` the cold aging recipes (beer, wine, whiskey and the rest) take only water of purity 3 or better. `[1.21.1]`
- Cold Sweat was never detected on 1.21.1: its mod id is `cold_sweat`, and Droplets of Thirst looked for `coldsweat`, so body temperature never drove thirst and never counted for hot dirty water. Both work now. Cold Sweat must be 2.4 or newer when installed; built against 2.4.3.1. `[1.21.1]`
- Cold Sweat: with `compat.toml` `coldsweat.useBodyTemperature` (default `true`), the climate multiplier of thirst now comes from the new `coldsweat.bodyTemperatureCurve` (default `["-100,0.8", "0,1.0", "50,1.3", "100,2.0", "150,3.0"]`: comfortable is 1, burning is twice the thirst). Before, the body temperature divided by 100 was fed into the biome formula, which made a comfortable body temperature halve thirst. The dimension's thirst multiplier and the Nether multiplier still come first. `[1.21.1]`
- Cold Sweat: drinking water can cool the body, through Cold Sweat's own temperature API: new `compat.toml` `coldsweat.drinkCooling` (default `0.0`, off) and `coldsweat.drinkCoolingTicks` (default `0`: a one-time drop of the body temperature; more: a lower base temperature for that long, like Cold Sweat's cold foods). It counts water containers, drinking by hand and the Traveler's Backpack hose; Cold Sweat's waterskin keeps its own water temperature instead. `[1.21.1]`
- Cold Sweat waterskin: drinking from it hydrates like a water drink (thirst 6, quenched 3, in `droplets_of_thirst:drinks`), with the purity of its water and its effects (thirst#210). Filling it from a water source, a water cauldron or a tank gives it the purity of that water. The furnace, campfire and smoker purification packs purify it like a bottle (thirst#267); it comes out full. An emptied waterskin (last sip, pouring, crafting) loses its purity, so it stacks with the other empty ones again. Both are small runtime mixins into Cold Sweat (`WaterskinItem.getFilledItem` and `FilledWaterskinItem.getCraftingRemainingItem`), because its waterskin copies every component between the empty and the filled item and only handles Thirst Was Taken's purity. Not done: Cold Sweat's boiler does not purify water for Droplets of Thirst (thirst#160), because it only knows Thirst Was Taken and changing that would mean patching Cold Sweat. `[1.21.1]`
- Create fans and basins purify water, never past clean (4), so pure water (5) still only comes from the Sand Filter: washing with a fan (water in front of it) takes water bottles and terracotta water bowls up one level per pass, up to clean; water buckets are left out, because Create hands back a bucket's crafting remainder next to the result. A mixer over a heated basin (Blaze Burner) takes water up one level per mix, up to clean, 250 mB per mix. Smoking and blasting fans already used the smoker and furnace purification recipes; with the smoker pack on, a blasting fan burns water containers like any food, as Create does. The new recipes carry `droplets_of_thirst:purity_enabled` and do not load with purity off. `[1.21.1]`
- Create spouts and drains keep purity: a spout filling a terracotta bowl gives it the purity of the water (before, it came out acceptable); the last water bottle of a stack emptied into a drain or tank keeps its purity (it was read after Create used the bottle up); terracotta water bowls can now be emptied in an Item Drain, keeping their purity. Emptying no longer writes the purity into the emptying recipe itself, which leaked it to the next item emptied with that recipe. `[1.21.1]`
- JEI (19.21 or newer, optional; also EMI when JEI is installed next to it, since EMI shows JEI plugins): new "Water Purification" page with the ways of purifying water that are not recipes, the water cauldron (murky water, clean over a heat source, with the heat sources listed) and, with Create, one Sand Filter step per purity with the configured amount and maximum. Cooking and Create recipes keep showing in their own pages. The page is not shown with `purity.enabled=false`. New "Hydration" page listing every item that changes thirst, with its droplets and values as the server resolved them (config, datapacks, estimated values marked as such). `[1.21.1]`
- Serene Seasons (10.1 or newer, optional): when Cold Sweat is not installed, the biome climate formula of thirst (`LEGACY` or `CURVE`) reads the biome's temperature as Serene Seasons changes it with the season, so thirst goes down where winter cools the biome. How much each sub-season changes the temperature is Serene Seasons' own `biome_temp_adjustment` in its `seasons.toml` (by default only winter, and the ends of spring and autumn, are colder; summer is as without seasons). Serene Seasons' temperature also cools with altitude, as vanilla does for snow, so high ground loses a bit less water than without it. Tropical biomes keep their heat all year and lose a little more water in their dry season: new `compat.toml` `sereneseasons.tropicalDrySeasonMultiplier` (default `1.1`; the wet season is 1). `sereneseasons.enabled` (default `true`) switches both off. Biomes without seasons (`sereneseasons:blacklisted_biomes`: oceans, rivers, beaches, caves), the Nether and a dimension's own thirst multiplier are left alone. With Cold Sweat nothing changes, since its body temperature already follows the seasons. Read once per second with the rest of the climate. Optional per-season multipliers of thirst loss, which do not change the temperature (`springMultiplier`, `summerMultiplier`, `autumnMultiplier`, `winterMultiplier`, default 1.0; `tropicalWetSeasonMultiplier`, default 1.0, next to the dry one) scale the climate further; with Serene Seasons' default config summer does not warm biomes, so `summerMultiplier` is how to make summer thirstier. `[1.21.1]`
- AppleSkin (client, optional): with the new `client.toml` option `followAppleSkin` (default `true`), turning off a food visual in AppleSkin's config also turns off its thirst counterpart (saturation outline → quenched outline, held-food preview and its off-hand option → drink preview, exhaustion underlay, tooltip values and its hold-Shift option → tooltip icons). The thirst tooltip row now sits right under AppleSkin's food row. `[1.21.1]`
- Traveler's Backpack (10.1 or newer, optional): drinking water with the hose hydrates like a water bottle (its drink values, so datapacks and the config apply), with the purity of the water in the tank and its effects. It uses Traveler's Backpack's own fluid effect API and replaces its water effect (putting out fire, regeneration in hot biomes), so a sip drains 250 mB, a bottle's worth, instead of 1000 mB. Water the hose sucks from the world keeps that water's purity (before, it read as `defaultPurity`, so a swamp gave acceptable water); water of another purity than the tank's is not mixed in. `[1.21.1]`
- AppleSkin is no longer used by any code: the thirst bar overlays and the thirst tooltip are built in, so they look the same with or without AppleSkin and there is nothing to break when AppleSkin updates. Supernatural blood items show the regular droplets in the tooltip. `[1.21.1]`
- Vampirism: vampires now see their thirst bar with blood-red droplets instead of no bar at all. Supernatural vampires get the same red droplets instead of Supernatural's thirst icon texture. `[1.21.1]`

Create must be 6.0.6 or newer when installed: older Create 6 versions drop fluid components from recipe outputs, so purity was lost (related to thirst#259). Built against Create 6.0.10. `[1.21.1]`

### Client
- Water in bottles and terracotta bowls is tinted by its purity, in the client only (the item does not change): brown (0), light brown (1), murky grey-green (2), vanilla blue (3), light blue (4), near white (5). Other potions and other mods' potion colors are untouched, and nothing is tinted with purity off. New client option `Purity tooltip.tintWaterByPurity` (default `true`). The terracotta water bowl model now has a separate liquid layer. `[1.21.1]`

- New client options `Thirst Bar.hideBarWhenFull` (default `false`) and `Thirst Bar.hideBarDelayTicks` (default `60`): the thirst bar is hidden while thirst is full, after the delay, and the air bar and other bars above it move down; it comes back as soon as thirst drops or you hold something that restores thirst. AppleSkin overlays are hidden with it (closes thirst#148, thirst#233)
- The thirst bar is now a regular HUD layer (`droplets_of_thirst:thirst_level`) drawn right above the hunger bar, instead of being drawn from inside the vanilla air bar through mixins. The air bar and bars from other mods stack above it, resource packs and HUD mods can move or hide it like any vanilla layer, and the `thirstBarXOffset`/`thirstBarYOffset` options still apply. `[1.21.1]`
- The thirst droplets change colour while a status applies, like vanilla hunger turns green under Hunger: brown with Dehydration, green with Poison, bright cyan with Quenchness, and blood red for vampires. Colours are set in `client.toml` (`Bar Colors`). `[1.21.1]`
- While Quenchness is active the droplets bounce one at a time across the bar, like hearts under Regeneration; the low-quenched shake still applies on top, as the low-health shake does for hearts (`Thirst Bar.buffWave`, on). `[1.21.1]`
- AppleSkin-style thirst HUD built in, no AppleSkin needed: an outline on the droplets shows quenched (`showQuenchedOverlay`, on), holding something that restores thirst (either hand) flashes the thirst and quenched it would give, estimated values included (`showDrinkPreview`, on), and an optional bar under the droplets shows exhaustion until the next point is lost (`showExhaustionUnderlay`, off). All three are drawn inside the thirst bar's layer, so they follow its position, shake, colour and hiding. With AppleSkin installed nothing is drawn twice: the old AppleSkin-only thirst overlays were removed (closes thirst#202, thirst#254). `[1.21.1]`
- Items that restore thirst show it in their tooltip as droplets and quenched icons, with `x N` past ten icons and `(est.)` for values estimated from recipes; this no longer needs AppleSkin and replaces the estimated-values text line (`Tooltip.showTooltipIcons`, on; off brings the text line back) (thirst#159). The client option `onlyShowPurityWhenShifting`, which did nothing, now hides the purity line until Shift is held. `[1.21.1]`

### Bug fixes

- `death.respawnThirst` and `respawnQuenched` are applied when the player respawns, not when they die: a death that another mod cancels (a totem of its own, a knock-down mechanic) no longer resets the thirst, and the death screen no longer shows the respawn values. Coming back from the End is not a death. The `ThirstChangeEvent` with cause `DEATH` is posted on the respawned player. `[1.21.1]`
- The Dehydration effect no longer piles up thirst exhaustion on creative players and on players with thirst disabled, which came due all at once when they went back to survival or thirst was enabled again. `[1.21.1]`
- OWN depletion mode: an attack costs thirst only when it lands, charged where vanilla charges hunger; a swing at an invulnerable target, or one that another mod cancels (claims, PvP rules), costs nothing. A block break that another mod cancels no longer costs thirst either. `[1.21.1]`
- Create Sand Filter: water below `sandFilterMbPerTick` left in the dirty tank (the last millibuckets a pipe delivers) is filtered too; before, it stayed there until more water arrived. `[1.21.1]`
- Create Sand Filter: Jade and other mods that ask for a block's fluid tanks without naming a side now see both of its tanks (dirty and purified) and their purity; before, they saw nothing. Water still only goes in at the back and out at the front. `[1.21.1]`
- Overhydration: a drink whose thirst change another mod cancels (`ThirstChangeEvent.Pre`) no longer counts towards Overhydrated; nothing was drunk. `[1.21.1]`
- Vampirism and Supernatural: Quenchness no longer hydrates vampires; like water and food, it is not blood. `[1.21.1]`
- `/droplets_of_thirst query` now returns the player's thirst (usable with `/execute store result`), `set` returns the thirst it set and `enable` the number of players; they all returned 0. `set` reports the values actually stored (quenched never above thirst, or what another mod's `ThirstChangeEvent` left), `enable` lists every player instead of only the first, and `set`/`enable` are shown to other operators like vanilla admin commands. `[1.21.1]`
- A purity component with a value outside 0-5 (other mods) now reads as the default purity when the item or fluid is loaded, so that water also stacks with water of the default purity; it no longer stays stored as an invalid number. Purity is sent to clients in one byte instead of four (network protocol version `0.1.12`). `[1.21.1]`
- The Create Ponder tag "Purification" showed its raw translation key; it now has a name and description. `[1.21.1]`
- AppleSkin: the quenched overlay and drink preview are no longer drawn on top of the chat, the F3 screen and other HUD elements, and they always line up with the thirst bar; before, they could end up at the bottom edge of the screen when AppleSkin's exhaustion underlay was turned off (closes thirst#223). `[1.21.1]`
- Campfires: a water container cooking on a campfire no longer hides the smoke of the other items on it; only its own slot gives off vapour instead of smoke. The vapour is now drawn by the vanilla particle code, so other mods that change campfire particles keep working while water is on it. `[1.21.1]`
- **Cauldrons no longer store a purity.** Droplets of Thirst no longer adds a `purity` property to the vanilla water cauldron block, which other mods and resource packs did not expect (closes thirst#247). The purity is decided when water leaves the cauldron (glass bottles, buckets, terracotta bowls, Create open pipe ends): always murky (2), or clean (4) while the cauldron stands on a heat source from the block tag `droplets_of_thirst:cauldron_heat_sources` (lit campfires, fire, soul fire, magma blocks and lava; datapacks can add more, blocks with a `lit` property only count while lit). A cauldron never gives purified water: that is what filters and purification recipes are for. What was poured in no longer matters, so the purity mixing of thirst#260 and thirst#170 is gone with it. It is applied by the cauldron, not by each item: any water container that comes out of a water cauldron interaction through `ItemUtils.createFilledResult` gets it, so other mods' containers registered in `CauldronInteraction.WATER` work without extra code, and the vanilla bottle and bucket interactions are no longer replaced. Pumps and pipes of other mods that use the NeoForge fluid capability on a cauldron see and drain water with the cauldron purity too, and can ask for water of that purity (NeoForge only accepted water without components). Terracotta bowls can now also be filled from a water cauldron. The drink gives that purity's effects. `[1.21.1]`

- Nausea, Farmer's Delight Nourishment, Let's Do Bakery Stuffed, Let's Do Brewery Saturated and Corail Tombstone Ghostly Shape now work whatever other effects the player has; before, only the first active effect was checked. Effects are matched by id (`farmersdelight:nourishment`, `bakery:stuffed`, `brewery:saturated`, `tombstone:ghostly_shape`), no longer by name fragments. `[1.21.1]`

- Drink, food and container lists (`item_settings.toml`, `container.toml`, `keyword.toml`) are read when the world loads and again on `/reload`, after tags are loaded: `#namespace:tag` entries now work and config edits apply with `/reload` instead of a restart (thirst#153, thirst#155). `[1.21.1]`
- Unknown item or tag ids in those lists are skipped instead of silently becoming air, with one warning per load (world load or `/reload`) that lists all of them (thirst#272); ids from mods that are not installed are only listed at debug level. `[1.21.1]`
- Asking for the thirst values of an item that has none returns 0 instead of crashing (thirst#239). `[1.21.1]`
- Reopening a singleplayer world no longer registers the config containers again. `[1.21.1]`
- Keyword matching (`keyword.toml`) runs once over all items when the lists are built, instead of on every tooltip and use; this also removes a rare crash when the client and the integrated server checked items at the same time. `[1.21.1]`
- `itemsBlacklist` now also removes items added by the config lists or by keywords, not only those added by other mods' code. `[1.21.1]`
- Looking at a water item's tooltip (or any other read of its purity) no longer adds a purity component to it. That write only happened on the client, so the stack could desync from the server and stop stacking with identical items (thirst#262, thirst#150, thirst#180, thirst#264). `[1.21.1]`
- Invalid purity values on items and fluids now read as `defaultPurity` ("acceptable" by default) instead of an invalid value. Items or fluids with no purity component read as `defaultPurity`, and both forms are accepted. `[1.21.1]`
- Quenched can no longer exceed thirst: drinking rain water, commands, syncing and loading old saves all keep `0 <= quenched <= thirst <= 20`. `[1.21.1]`

- No thirst packets are sent to fake players (machines and automation from other mods) or to players without a connection; this could crash with some mods. `[1.21.1]`
- Dispensers with an empty bucket facing a water block that cannot be picked up (for example kelp, or a modded block holding water) now use the vanilla behaviour instead of crashing. `[1.21.1]`
- A potion item without potion contents no longer crashes the water bottle stack size check. `[1.21.1]`
- **Dehydration on Normal difficulty no longer kills**: like vanilla starvation it stops at half a heart. Easy still stops at 5 hearts and Hard can still kill. `[1.21.1]`
- Only the Fire Protection enchantment on armor slows thirst loss; generic Protection no longer counts (thirst#195, thirst#219). The reduction per level and the 12-level cap are unchanged. `[1.21.1]`
- The "no sprinting when thirsty" rule now follows the server's `moveSlowWhenThirsty`, sent to the client with the thirst data; before, each client read its own config, so a client could turn it off. Sprinting also checks thirst and food separately, so exactly 6 food no longer lets you sprint when thirst is high. `[1.21.1]`
- The server now also checks that the hands are empty before drinking by hand. `[1.21.1]`
- Water purity is now always stored on items and fluids, also for the default purity ("acceptable"). Before, the default purity was removed, so the campfire and furnace purification recipes for buckets, bottles and terracotta bowls never matched water filled from an acceptable cauldron or tank (thirst#259). Those recipes now also accept water with no purity stored (old saves, vanilla sources), which reads as the default purity. `[1.21.1]`

The old code kept the purity in shared fields of the bucket and bottle items, so in singleplayer the client and the integrated server could swap each other's purity. Bottles of dragon's breath no longer get a purity. Buckets from other mods that reuse the vanilla bucket code no longer get purity from the world (they read as the default purity). `[1.21.1]`
- Drinking water rolls the purity effects once (before, water bottles rolled twice, thirst#204) and always hydrates once per drink, also when drinking from a stack of bottles or the last bottle (thirst#209). Water with no purity stored now gives the effects of the default purity instead of none. Purity effects and hydration from water containers and non-food drinks are handled on the server only, for players only: villagers, witches and other mobs drinking potions are no longer touched (related to thirst#203). Drinks consumed without the use animation (for example by automatic feeding from other mods) only hydrate if they are food. `[1.21.1]`
- **Drinking by hand has a cooldown** (`handDrinkingCooldown`, 10 ticks by default) and is fully checked by the server: `canDrinkByHand`, sneaking, empty hands, reach, spawn protection and that the player really looks at water (the server casts its own ray; the client no longer sends a position). Before, a modified client could drink unlimited water from anywhere, one click sent two drinks (one per hand), and water at negative coordinates was looked up one block off. The drinking sound now comes from the server and is heard by the player and those nearby. `[1.21.1]`
- On a dedicated server, clients now use the server's drink and food values, purity containers, blacklist and `defaultPurity` for tooltips, AppleSkin and the HUD, instead of their own config files (thirst#274, thirst#262, thirst#153). They are sent when joining and again after `/reload`, and dropped when leaving the server. Vanilla water bottles no longer show a purity different from the server's. `[1.21.1]`
- A potion item without potion contents no longer crashes item tooltips or campfire particles. Thirst no longer ticks (and sends no updates) while the player is dead. `[1.21.1]`

### Performance

- Thirst data is sent to the player only when thirst, quenched, the exhaustion shown by AppleSkin (in steps of 0.1) or a synced rule changes, at most once per tick, plus a full resync every 10 seconds and after respawning or changing dimension. Before, every player got one packet per tick while thirst was ticking. A player standing still now gets almost no thirst packets. `[1.21.1]`
- The thirst loss multiplier from climate (biome temperature and humidity, or Cold Sweat body temperature; Nether), Fire Protection and Fire Resistance is computed once per second per player (players are spread over different ticks), and right away after changing armor, gaining or losing an effect, changing dimension or respawning. Before, it was computed up to three times per tick. Walking into another biome, or a Cold Sweat temperature change, now takes effect within one second. `[1.21.1]`
- Whether the player has Nausea (`depletion.nauseaDepletes`) is read when their effects change, together with `stops_thirst` and `pauses_thirst`, instead of being looked up every tick. `[1.21.1]`
- Checking whether an item is a water container with purity (tooltips, drinking, campfires, Create filling) is one map lookup instead of a scan over all containers. `[1.21.1]`
- The thirst bar always reads the current player's thirst; before, it kept the data of the previous player object for up to 2 seconds after respawning or changing dimension. The thirst bar and the AppleSkin overlays and tooltip no longer create objects every frame. `[1.21.1]`
- Purity effects use the player's random generator instead of creating a new one for every drink. `[1.21.1]`
- Create Sand Filter: an idle filter (not enough dirty water, or a full purified tank) does no fluid work at all each tick. `[1.21.1]`
- The purity line of a water container's tooltip is built once per language instead of on every frame the tooltip is shown. `[1.21.1]`
- The tooltip line of estimated values (shown when the tooltip icons are off) is built when the values change, not on every frame. `[1.21.1]`

### Effects

- New mechanic, on by default: drinking murky or dirtier water (purity 2 or less, `hotDirtyWater.maxPurity`) in a hot climate, from a bottle or by hand, also gives **Dehydration** for 30 seconds on top of the usual purity effects. Hot means the Nether, a biome with temperature 1.0 or more (deserts, savanna, badlands; not jungle), or, with Cold Sweat, a body temperature above 50. Configure or disable it with `hotDirtyWater.enabled` and the other `[hotDirtyWater]` keys in `purity.toml`. `PurityEffectEvent` sees the effect and can cancel or change it. `[1.21.1]`
- New **Dehydration** effect (`droplets_of_thirst:dehydration`), the thirst counterpart of Hunger: while active it drains thirst faster, 0.005 exhaustion per tick per level, scaled by `effects.dehydrationMultiplier` (`gameplay.toml`, default 1.0). Nothing applies it by default; use `/effect give @player droplets_of_thirst:dehydration 30 1` or add it to the purity effect lists in `purity.toml`. Thirst never goes below zero (idea from Thirst Was Reclaimed; unlike theirs it never drives thirst below zero). `[1.21.1]`
- **Quenchness** (`droplets_of_thirst:quenchness`) is now a regular effect like Regeneration: every 2 seconds (`effects.quenchnessIntervalTicks`, `gameplay.toml`, default 40 ticks) it restores one thirst and one quenched per level, through the normal drink path (clamped, `ThirstChangeEvent` with cause `DRINK`, `extraThirstToQuenched` applies). Before, it was an instant effect that restored one point every tick for as long as it lasted. It now has its name in every language and a watery blue colour. `[1.21.1]`
- New **Potion of Quenchness** (`droplets_of_thirst:quenchness`, 0:45), long (`long_quenchness`, 1:30, + redstone) and strong (`strong_quenchness`, Quenchness II 0:22, + glowstone), with splash, lingering and tipped arrow variants. Brewed from an awkward potion with **prismarine crystals** (no vanilla potion uses them). `effects.quenchnessPotion` (default `true`) turns the recipes off; keep it the same on the server and the clients. `[1.21.1]`
- New **Hydrated** effect (`droplets_of_thirst:hydrated`): thirst goes down slower, all thirst exhaustion times `effects.hydratedMultiplier` (default 0.5) per level. The thirst bar waves and turns mint green while it is active (`Bar Colors.hydrated`). Nothing gives it by default: use `/effect`, the purity effect lists, or the new option below. `[1.21.1]`
- New option, off by default: `hydration.fullBonus` gives Hydrated I while you stay fully hydrated (thirst 20 and at least `minQuenched` quenched, default 10) for `fullBonusSeconds` (30); it is refreshed while that holds and lasts `fullBonusDurationTicks` (200) after (closes thirst#186). `[1.21.1]`
- New option, off by default: `effects.waterBreathingReducesThirst`: fully underwater with Water Breathing or Conduit Power, thirst loss is multiplied by `effects.underwaterBreathingMultiplier` (0.5), on top of `climate.underwater`. `[1.21.1]`
- New mechanic, on by default: **overhydration**. Thirst and quenched drunk past full (bottles, food, hand drinking, rain, `DropletsAPI.drink`; not Quenchness) add up as overflow, which drains by `overhydration.decayPerSecond` (1.0) points per second. At `overhydration.threshold` (20, about two water bottles while full) the player gets the new harmful **Overhydrated** effect (`droplets_of_thirst:overhydrated`, 10% slower movement per level) for `durationTicks` (400), one level more per half threshold past it or when it is already active (at most III), plus 10 seconds of Nausea (`overhydration.nausea`). The thirst bar turns greyish blue while it lasts (`Bar Colors.overhydrated`). Disable it with `overhydration.enabled = false` (`gameplay.toml`). `[1.21.1]`
- Dirty water keeps giving the vanilla Nausea, Hunger and Poison from the purity lists in `purity.toml`; there is no separate water sickness effect. `[1.21.1]`

### API

- Negative (salty) values: `ThirstValues.saltiness()` and `DrinkEvent#getSaltiness()`/`isSalty()` give the thirst points an item removes (2 points = 1 droplet on the HUD). The `drinks` data map codec now allows thirst -20..20 and quenched ≥ -20. Network protocol version `0.1.11` (a stack-dependent entry in `droplets_of_thirst:thirst_values` is now marked with thirst `Integer.MIN_VALUE` instead of -1). `[1.21.1]`
- Eating and drinking are now separate hooks. Food (items without the drink animation that are not water containers, also when another mod calls `Player#eat`, block foods and the new `DropletsAPI.eat(player, thirst, quenched)`) posts the new `EatEvent.Pre` (cancel, or change thirst and quenched) and `EatEvent.Post`, and changes thirst with the new `ThirstChangeEvent.Cause.EAT`; drinks (drink animation: potions, milk, honey bottles, most modded drinks; water containers; hand drinking; `DropletsAPI.drink`) keep `DrinkEvent` and `Cause.DRINK`. Both events have `getSaltiness()`/`isSalty()`. Food no longer rolls purity effects: a `purity` in `droplets_of_thirst:drinks` only applies to drinks. `[1.21.1]`
- New cancelable `OverhydrationEvent`: posted before Overhydrated is applied, with the overflow; change the duration or the amplifier, or cancel. `[1.21.1]`
- `DropletsAPI.registerWaveEffect(effect)` makes the thirst bar wave while the local player has that effect (client side), for positive thirst effects. `[1.21.1]`
- `DropletsAPI.registerBarStyle(id, predicate, rgb, priority)` tints the thirst bar while the predicate holds for the local player (client side); see the wiki page Mod Developers. Removed `ThirstBarRenderer.cancelRender` and the copied GlitchCore `RenderGuiEvent`; the bar is the GUI layer `droplets_of_thirst:thirst_level`, which other mods can move or cancel with NeoForge's `RenderGuiLayerEvent`. `[1.21.1]`
- New wiki page Mod Developers: dependency setup, soft-dependency guard, reading, changing, registering and events, with examples. `[1.21.1]`
- Events on the NeoForge bus (`com.darkona.dropletsofthirst.api.event`), server side and only when something happens, never every tick: `ThirstChangeEvent.Pre` (cancel or change the new values) and `.Post`, with a cause (depletion, drink, rain, Peaceful, death, command, API); `DrinkEvent.Pre` (cancel or change thirst, quenched and purity) and `.Post` (with whether it hydrated), for items, drinking by hand and `DropletsAPI.drink`; `PurityEffectEvent` (cancel, or edit the rolled effects and whether the drink still hydrates); `DehydrationDamageEvent` (cancel or change the damage). Eating a drink-food no longer updates thirst on the client ahead of the server; the server's value arrives the same tick. `[1.21.1]`
- Registering from code: `DropletsAPI.registerDrink(item, thirst, quenched[, purity])`, `registerDrinkProvider(item, provider)` for values that depend on the stack's components, `registerContainer([empty,] filled)` for water containers with purity, and `registerExhaustionModifier(id, modifier)` for thirst loss rules the `droplets_of_thirst:thirst_drain` attribute cannot express (evaluated with the cached multiplier about once a second, in id order; `refreshExhaustionModifier(player)` forces it). Registered values are applied every time the tables are built; `items.toml`, `droplets_of_thirst:no_thirst` and the `droplets_of_thirst:drinks` data map still win over them, and they win over keywords and recipe estimates. `RegisterThirstValueEvent` and `WaterPurity.addContainer` are deprecated in favour of these. `/droplets_of_thirst debug exhaustion` shows the registered modifiers as "other mods". The impl class `ExhaustionModifier` is now `ExhaustionFactors`. Network protocol version `0.1.10`. `[1.21.1]`

Server-side changes: `setThirst`, `setQuenched`, `addThirst`, `drink(player, thirst, quenched[, purity])` (rolls purity effects like a bottle) and `addExhaustion` (multiplied like the mod's own activities); on a client they do nothing and log one warning. ### For developers

- Mixins no longer replace whole methods of Minecraft, NeoForge or Create, so they combine with other mods that hook the same code: bucket purity through the NeoForge fluid capability is added to the result of `FluidBucketWrapper.getFluid` and `FluidType.getBucket(FluidStack)` instead of overwriting `getFluid` and `FluidUtil.getFilledBucket`; dispensers give water containers the purity of the water in front of them from `DefaultDispenseItemBehavior.consumeWithRemainder`, so the vanilla bucket and glass bottle dispenser behaviours are no longer replaced (other mods that register their own keep working in any load order) and containers dispensed through NeoForge's `DispenseFluidContainer` get it too. The `@Redirect`s on regeneration in `FoodData.tick`, on the empty bottle in `PotionItem.finishUsingItem` and on the food level in `LocalPlayer.hasEnoughFoodToStartSprinting` are now `@WrapOperation`/`@ModifyExpressionValue`, with the same behaviour. Filling a bucket or a glass bottle from water in the world is hooked into `BucketItem.use` and `BottleItem.use` (the filled item gets the purity) instead of cancelling every right click with those items and doing vanilla's work again, so other mods' buckets and bottles that extend them get purity too; the terracotta bowl has its own item class. Drinking a terracotta water bowl hands back the empty bowl through vanilla's `ItemUtils.createFilledResult`, like a glass bottle; in creative mode you now get an empty bowl if you have none, as with bottles. Create goggles show the purity by wrapping the one call that names the fluid in `IHaveGoggleInformation.containedFluidTooltip`, instead of overwriting the whole method; the rest of the tooltip is Create's own again. `[1.21.1]`
- The jar needs Minecraft 1.21.1 and NeoForge 21.1.252 or newer. `[1.21.1]`
