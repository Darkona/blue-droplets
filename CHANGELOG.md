# Changelog

All notable changes to Droplets of Thirst are in this file. The format follows [Keep a Changelog](https://keepachangelog.com/en/1.1.0/), and each entry starts with the date of the change.

Upstream issues are cited as `thirst#NN` (ghen-git/Thirst-Mod). The tag at the end of each entry, such as `[1.21.1]` or `[26.3]`, names the Minecraft port of the change. None of these ports is released yet.

The 1.21.1 port (`droplets-of-thirst-1.21.1-1.0.0.jar`) is the base. The 26.1 port (`droplets-of-thirst-26.1-1.0.0.jar`, Minecraft 26.1.2 and NeoForge 26.1.2.109 or newer) has every feature of 1.21.1 that does not depend on a mod without a 26.1 build. The 26.2 port (`droplets-of-thirst-26.2-1.0.0.jar`, NeoForge 26.2.0.88 or newer) has every feature of the 26.1 port. The 26.3 port (`droplets-of-thirst-26.3-1.0.0.jar`, Minecraft 26.3 and NeoForge 26.3.0.36-beta only) has every feature of the 26.2 port except the Reliquary integration.

Planned ports, each one after the previous version in the chain is stable: 26.3 (NeoForge), 1.20.1 (one jar for Forge 47.x and NeoForge 47.1), 1.19.2 (Forge), 1.18.2 (Forge), 1.12.2 (Forge) and 1.7.10 (Forge).

- 2026-07-03: **Poured water keeps its purity.** Sea water poured into a meadow no longer comes back as meadow water when you fill a bucket there. The mod remembers a water source poured with a bucket (by a player or a dispenser), placed through `FluidUtil.tryPlaceFluid` of NeoForge (which the containers of many mods use) or placed by a Create open pipe end, with the purity of the poured water (or `defaultPurity` when it had none). Bottles, bowls and buckets filled there, drinking by hand, Create pipes and hose pulleys read that purity. The infinite source that forms between two poured sources takes the worst purity beside it. When you pick up a poured source, it hands its purity to the sources beside it, so the one that refills its place is not clean either. A remembered position that no longer holds a water source is ignored and forgotten when read. The data is one small map per chunk, saved with the chunk and never sent to clients. Nothing runs per tick. `[1.21.1]`

Deserts and badlands start at 0 with a cap of 2, and swamps and mangroves start at 0 with a cap of 1 (the last matching entry wins, so swamps come last).
